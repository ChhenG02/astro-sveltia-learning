## Project Overview

To support expanding clean-water demand in Ta Khmau, PATech upgraded and expanded the plant's supervisory control and data acquisition (SCADA) architecture to manage a daily production capacity of 30,000 m³/day.

### Key Engineering Highlights

- **Centralized SCADA Visibility**: Unified intake pumps, filtration beds, clear-well levels, and distribution headers onto a single operator workstation.
- **Automated Chemical Dosing**: Integrated flow-paced dosing control so coagulant and chlorine rates adjust automatically to real-time throughput.
- **High-Reliability Switchgear**: Assembled and tested moisture-protected local control panels with manual/auto bypass modes for zero-downtime maintenance.
