## Project Overview

PATech partnered with De Heus TMH Feed Mill to deploy a 200 kW AC industrial solar photovoltaic system integrated directly into the plant's low-voltage distribution network.

### Technical Scope

- **Industrial Solar Array & Inverters**: High-efficiency PV strings engineered for industrial rooftop wind loads and tropical heat dissipation.
- **Switchboard & Protection Integration**: Custom AC combiner and synchronization panel built by PATech to protect plant generators from back-feeding.
- **Energy Management Telemetry**: Real-time monitoring of solar contribution versus plant motor load.
