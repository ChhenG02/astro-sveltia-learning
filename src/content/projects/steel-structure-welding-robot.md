## Project Overview

PATech engineered, supplied, and commissioned a turnkey robotic beam welding system for a leading steel structure fabrication plant in Phnom Penh.

### What We Delivered

- **Mechanical & Track Installation**: Precision leveling and mounting of the linear rail system to handle full-length structural H-beams and I-beams.
- **Welding Parameter Tuning**: Calibrated current, voltage, wire feed speed, and weave patterns for varying plate thicknesses.
- **Safety & Operator Training**: Installed perimeter light curtains and trained the shop-floor team on fixture loading, program selection, and daily torch maintenance.
