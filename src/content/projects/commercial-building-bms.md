## Project Overview

PATech designed, fabricated, and commissioned a 5000A Main Switchboard (MSB) for K-Cement's heavy industrial facility in Kampot Province.

### Engineering & Quality Assurance

- **In-House Fabrication**: Assembled at PATech Nano Factory using precision-punched copper busbars and certified low-voltage switchgear components.
- **Factory Acceptance Testing (FAT)**: Executed dielectric, insulation resistance, and secondary injection relay tests prior to dispatch.
- **On-Site Energization**: Coordinated shutdown window, cable termination torque verification, and 24/7 post-energization standby support.
